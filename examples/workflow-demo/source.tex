% Original teaching fixture, authored for this workflow demonstration.
% This is not an excerpt from the probability textbook or a catalog task.
For natural numbers $h$ (observed hits) and $n$ (observations), define the
integer per-thousand empirical frequency as $\lfloor 1000h/n\rfloor$.
The interface must require $0<n$ and $h\leq n$. It is deliberately a scaled,
rounded integer, not an exact real-valued probability.

The caller for two hits in four observations must supply both conditions
and prove that its result is $500$ by evaluating the definition.
